About DivyaChetana Organics LLP

Faith, purity, creativity, and service

Our Story

DivyaChetana Organics LLP was born from a belief that saatvik living should be accessible to every household in India. Founded in 2026, we bridge traditional spiritual practices and modern convenience.

What started as delivering handcrafted organic puja samagri has grown into a multi-vertical LLP:

  • Saatvik Puja Items (B2C): 100% pure organic puja items delivered pan-India
  • Digital Content & Marketing (B2B): Video, social media, brand strategy for spiritual brands
  • Event Management (B2B): Grand spiritual events, corporate programs, cultural festivals

🙏 "We nurture a movement toward conscious, saatvik living."

Mission & Vision

Make saatvik living accessible. Deliver pure organic puja items. Support spiritual communities with digital excellence. Operate with full compliance and transparency.
