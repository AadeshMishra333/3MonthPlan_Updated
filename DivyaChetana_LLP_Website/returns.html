Return & Refund Policy

Fair, transparent, hassle-free returns

Last Updated: 1 June 2026. Compliant with Consumer Protection Act 2019 & E-Commerce Rules 2020.

1. Return Eligibility

  • Product damaged/defective on delivery
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Quality doesn't match description
  • Request within 7 days of delivery

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable puja items (flowers, prasad)
  • Customized/personalized items
  • Opened/used items
  • Gift cards

3. How to Return

  • Email support@divyachetana.com with order number + photos
  • Phone: +91 98765 43210 (Mon–Sat 9AM–7PM)
  • My Account → Order History → Request Return
  • Review within 2 business days

4. Refund Process

  • Inspection: 3–5 business days after receipt
  • Refund to original payment method
  • UPI: 1–3 days · Cards: 5–10 days · Net Banking: 5–7 days

5. Damaged in Transit

Photo/video within 24hrs → free replacement or full refund.

6. Contact

Email: support@divyachetana.com · Phone: +91 98765 43210 · Mon–Sat 9AM–7PM IST
